## OTA engineering assessment

The DMA buffer is allocated and initialized before BLE/Wi-Fi even when the panels are physically disconnected, and remains active through upload (single buffer, no added render allocation). Bench heap/stack data is therefore relevant to resource accounting but doesn't test the panel supply under load. BLE maintenance admission still stops advertising with a quiet interval, refuses a connected controller and cancels on unexpected BLE activity. Device upload and actual flash-begin still enforce 49,152 B free heap and 4,096 B contiguous internal 8-bit heap; signed signature/header checks, inactive-slot selection, complete readback and trial watchdog/deferred confirmation are unchanged. No power or display sequencing changes: boot blanks OE during driver setup (not a bootloader-wide guarantee) and checks display, BLE, OTA and loop progress before confirming a trial. A transient display blank/reset on install is expected; brownout, repeated reset and persistent flicker are not. Neither a healthy image nor an accepted `/api/close` proves power/thermal margins, true radio teardown or later stability. Earlier Wi-Fi shutdown warnings and heap drift remain open.

New authenticated status exposes `heap`, `minimumHeap`, `largestInternal8` and `httpStackFree`. The last value is the HTTP task's historical high-water mark in bytes, not a live multi-task inventory. Older source images only expose current heap over HTTP, so their exact upload stack watermark and contiguous measurements remain unavailable without serial; report them unavailable, not inferred. Do not attach serial with the panels connected to fill a telemetry gap.
