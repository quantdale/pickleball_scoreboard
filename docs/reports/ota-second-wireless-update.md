## Why the monitor stopped

The external one-shot guard wrongly paired `SW_CPU_RESET` with `0x3`. Pinned ESP32 ROM `rtc.h` defines `SW_RESET=3` and **`SW_CPU_RESET=12` (`0xc`)**. The first wireless update also logged `0xc`. Offline replay checked the exact candidate install → single expected reset → pending trial → confirmation ordering and verified that a pre-install/extra reset, wrong reason, missing confirmation or firmware fault is rejected. Four replay tests passed. No original runner output was altered; the correction is in the offline audit. The UART observation supports a confirmed trial, but cannot substitute for the independently authenticated post-boot digest and health check.

BLE recovery after AP close could not be checked because there was no verified close. No new heap or stack sample was available; the transfer measurements above are from the prior installation. No score commands, uploads, staging, installs, firmware/metadata writes, or reset commands occurred.

The repository now has a hardware-free reset classifier and four regression tests (`tools/ota_boot_evidence.py`, `tools/test_ota_boot_evidence.py`). The classifier accepts one install-correlated `0xc (SW_CPU_RESET)`, rejects the old `0x3` pairing and fault/extra-reset cases, and explicitly does not attest boot health. ## Post-maintenance BLE recovery

**PASS.** In the single authorized BLE-only check, Windows passively observed the pinned Court 1 BLE address `3c:8a:1f:a1:ba:2a`. One connection confirmed the PickleScore GATT service (`4fafc201-1fb5-459e-8fcc-c5c9c331914b`) and State Notify characteristic (`…26a9`). Initial Notify delivered `0,0,L,2,0,D` without a scoring command. The client unsubscribed and disconnected normally; a new passive scan observed advertising again 3.379 s after disconnect, without a second connection. This establishes operational BLE recovery after the previous maintenance session, not the exact time it recovered after AP close.

Windows Wi-Fi remained disconnected with the same hardware/software radio state; Ethernet source-bound TLS succeeded before and after. Apart from the GATT notification subscription and unsubscribe, there were no BLE writes, maintenance activations, scoring commands, reboots, firmware/metadata changes, or COM11 access. The check did not re-read firmware over HTTP; its exact identity and health were independently verified in the preceding session.

Taken together, the close acknowledgment, driver link loss, absence of a fresh AP timestamp after close, and successful BLE recovery are operationally consistent with AP shutdown. Independent RF or SDK deinitialization verification was not performed and is not an acceptance blocker absent a functional or security defect. No reinstall, new maintenance session, or packet capture is needed for this checkpoint.
