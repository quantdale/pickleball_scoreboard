## 1. Reconnect and power up

The owner must first confirm the court is idle, no score needs preserving, ESP32 USB is unplugged, the panel source is isolated, and its DC output and both panels are discharged. **Do not reconnect without this confirmation.**

1. Check the existing approved topology against the [power prerequisites](../../hardware-tests/p10-bringup/README.md#additional-physical-safety-gate-12v-supply--buck-converter). Use the unchanged regulated **5 V** protected panel branches and common ground. Where the 12 V supply feeds a buck, 12 V goes only to that converter. A competent person must verify output voltage/polarity against the actual panel rating, branch protection, current/cooling ratings and enclosed mains arrangements before energizing. If anything is unknown or the power path changed, stop. Do not adjust the converter with the load attached.
2. With **both** sources off and discharged: ESP32 → **Panel 1 J1** in the already verified ribbon orientation; **Panel 1 J2 → Panel 2 J1**; **Panel 2 J2 empty**. Panel 1 is the existing left/controller-nearest panel. Do not reverse ribbons, reroute GPIO or modify the chain-block mapping. Each panel remains on its own existing protected 5 V branch. Keep the approved ground path; no panel load current through the ESP32 or a signal jumper. The ESP32 is **USB-powered only**, with no panel/buck positive on its power pins.
3. With everything secured, energize panel 5 V and controller USB in close succession using their normal controls. Avoid prolonged one-sided power and **never hot-plug HUB75**. At **12/255**, expect upright white `0–0` and two left arrows across the 64×16 display; no right arrows. Record a photo. For unexpected brightness/flicker, excessive heat, smell, brownout, or any wiring doubt, switch off both sources and wait for discharge. Do not probe live mains or rely on the brightness setting as current protection.

## 3. Bounded endurance with the owner present

After the functional sequence, keep the same wiring and brightness. Run **20 minutes of ordinary scoring in four 5-minute blocks** (maintenance and reconnect checkpoints may extend wall time). No live match or continuous button spam. Alternate normal doubles and singles play, include a two-digit display, and record at minutes 0/5/10/15/20: score/serve and panel/preview agreement, missing or duplicate actions, delays, link changes, flicker/corruption, and any unexpected score reset or restart. Include a controlled mid-run app reconnect and one at completion. Do not power-cycle to hide a fault.

- **Maintenance:** At two comparable idle checkpoints on the same warmed boot (start and finish, after the boot AP is closed and with the phone explicitly disconnected), use the [verified no-write workflow](ota-second-wireless-update.md#ap-readiness-gated-verification) only with the owner supervising and permitting each idle window: one authenticated activation, wait for the fresh pinned BSSID, sign in, read exact image/uptime and current heap at the same authenticated status stage, close, then reconnect the phone and confirm the retained score. No upload/install requests. Record close acknowledgment, driver link loss, BLE recovery and Windows network restoration. If this is not possible, record maintenance/heap as NOT TESTED, not PASS.
- **Diagnostics:** Compare current heap and increasing uptime at equivalent checkpoints; below the existing 49,152 B operation admission gate, an admission rejection, or an unexpected uptime reset is a failure. A two-point sample is not proof of no long-term leak. If safe serial telemetry is unavailable, report internal largest block, historical minimum, stack watermarks and watchdog reason as not measured for this run. Do not open/reset COM11 with panels attached to obtain them.
- **Electrical/thermal:** At settled start and end (and any safe observed transient), a competent owner records DC voltage at each panel input within manufacturer tolerances, branch/total current only with suitable rated equipment already fitted safely, and ambient/supply/converter/controller/connector temperatures where safely measurable. Use insulated low-voltage test points; power off and discharge before adding or moving any instruments. No exposed live probes at mains, improvised inline ammeter, load increase, or brightness change. Compare against actual continuous ratings and temperature limits under the intended installation conditions. If ratings/equipment are not available, record the missing evidence; electrical acceptance stays open.
- **Stop on first fault:** wrong score after a confirmed command, repeated missed sync, visible corruption, reset/watchdog, unexplained loss of power, voltage outside limits, abnormal connector heating, smell, or unsafe conditions. Preserve the first failing step and observation. Cut power for safety faults. No automatic retest, reset or change to the hardware.
